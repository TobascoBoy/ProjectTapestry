Tapestry Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 20, 2026
Who we are: Luke Richard Hattrup, doing business as Tapestry ("Tapestry", "we").
Contact: privacy@jointhetapestry.com · 1292 High St, Unit # A3258, Eugene, OR 97401

This policy explains what we collect when you use the Tapestry app and website, why, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and your choices.

The short version: we collect what's needed to run your account and show your scraps to the people you choose. We don't sell your data, we don't show ads, and we don't track you across other companies' apps or websites.

1. What we collect

Account information

  • Your email address, or your Sign in with Apple identifier. If you use Apple's "Hide My Email", we only get the relay address.
  • Your name, username, pronouns, bio and profile photo, if you add them.
  • The version of our Terms you accepted, and when.

Your content

  • Photos and videos you turn into scraps, the scraps themselves, and the tiles and tapestries you make.
  • The messages you write in chats.
  • Your friends and friend requests, the chats you are in and what is sent in them, reactions, gold stars, whether you have opened a chat, the members of tapestries you share, and your streak and XP.

Finding friends from your contacts (optional)

  • If you tap "Find friends from Contacts", the app reads the e-mail addresses in the address book on your phone, scrambles each one on the phone (a one-way hash that cannot be turned back into the address), and sends only those scrambled values to our server to see which belong to Tapestry accounts. Names, phone numbers and the addresses themselves never leave your phone, and the scrambled values are not stored after the check. Nothing is read until you tap, and iOS asks your permission first.
  • If you invite a contact, we keep the scrambled form of their e-mail address with the invite, so we can connect the two of you when they join. It is deleted with the invite if the invite is cancelled or expires.

Safety records

  • Reports you make or that are made about your content, people you block, and actions we take (for example removing a scrap or banning an account).

Device and usage information

  • Push notification token, so we can send the notifications you allow.
  • App analytics through Google Firebase Analytics. We log that certain actions happened, never what was in them: signing up and signing in; the steps of first-time setup (including your answer to the Contacts question and how many contacts matched, as counts only); taking or choosing a photo or video, cutting something out, and which editing tools you opened; saving a scrap; sending a scrap or a message (how many, and whether to one person or a group, never the content or the recipient); collecting, starring or reacting; sending or accepting a friend request or an invite; opening a chat; creating, joining, opening or deleting a tapestry; adding a tile or placing a scrap on one; and tapping a notification or a widget. These are linked to your account ID, not your name or email.
  • Device details that come with analytics and crash reports: your phone model, iOS version, app version, language, an identifier for this installation of the app, an identifier Apple gives our apps on your phone (the "identifier for vendor"), and a general location such as your city or country, which Google works out from your IP address. We don't collect your precise location, and we don't use the advertising identifier (IDFA).
  • Crash reports through Google Firebase Crashlytics: technical details of a crash and your account ID, so we can fix it.
  • Server and abuse-prevention logs: like any online service, our servers log requests, including your IP address. We also record your account ID and IP address on some actions (for example searching, sending, matching contacts, creating or opening a friend invite), so we can rate-limit abuse.

If you write to us, we keep your email and what you tell us.

On our website, we use no analytics and set no cookies. The company that hosts the site keeps ordinary server logs, the site loads its fonts from Google, and a friend-invite page checks your IP address briefly to stop abuse.

What stays on your phone. Cutting a subject out of a photo happens on your phone; the original photo isn't uploaded for that. The nudity check that runs before an upload also happens on your phone: it looks at whether a picture shows a person and whether it looks explicit, it doesn't identify anyone or create a face template, and nothing from it is sent to us.

What we don't collect: your address book (see "Finding friends" above — only scrambled e-mail addresses are checked, and only when you ask), your precise location, your browsing history, or photos you don't choose. The camera, microphone and photo library are used only when you use them.

2. Why we use it

  • To run the Service: your account, your collection, chats, shared tiles, home-screen widgets and notifications.
  • To keep people safe: reports, blocks, automated checks, moderation and enforcing our Terms.
  • To fix and improve the app, using crash reports and the analytics events above.
  • To answer you when you write to us.
  • To meet legal obligations.

We don't sell or rent your personal information, we don't share it for advertising, and we don't use it to build a profile of you for anyone else.

3. Who can see what

  • Your profile (name, username, photo, bio, pronouns, and your streak and XP) can be seen by other Tapestry users, who can find you by searching your name or username.
  • People who have your e-mail address in their phone's contacts can find your Tapestry account if they use "Find friends from Contacts" and you signed up with that address.
  • Your tapestries are visible only to their members — the people in the chat a tapestry belongs to who have joined it, and you alone for one you make by yourself.
  • Scraps and messages you send are seen by everyone in the chat you send them to, who can collect them, react to them and place them on tiles inside Tapestry (see the Terms). They can see whether you have read what they sent. Someone who joins a group chat later sees only what is sent after they join. In a group chat, the other members can see your name and photo even if you aren't friends with them.
  • A friend invite link shows whoever opens it your first name and how many scraps are waiting — never the scraps themselves.
  • Reported content is reviewed by Tapestry staff, including scraps and messages sent privately, when someone reports them. That's how reports work.

Chats on Tapestry are private to their members but are not end-to-end encrypted: they are stored on our servers so they can be delivered, and we can access them when a report or the law requires it. We don't read them otherwise.

6. Deleting your account

Settings → Delete Account deletes your account immediately. It removes:

  • your profile and profile photo;
  • tapestries you own and everything on them, for everyone;
  • scraps you placed on other people's tapestries;
  • your scrap collection;
  • the scraps and messages you sent, and your reactions and stars;
  • your friendships and your one-to-one chats (for the other person too), your place in every group chat, your notifications, your streak and your blocks.

What stays:

  • A scrap you sent that someone else has already placed on one of their tiles or kept in their own collection stays with them, as the Terms explain. If you want one removed, write to us.
  • Reports and moderation records, kept for the periods above. A report you made stays, without your name on it.
  • Analytics and crash records already sent to Google aren't erased at once; they expire on the schedule in section 5.
  • Copies in backups, for up to 30 days.

If you signed in with Apple, deleting your account asks you to confirm with Apple once more, so that we can also end Tapestry's access to your Apple sign-in. You can check or remove it yourself under "Sign in with Apple" in your Apple Account settings. If you can't get into the app, email privacy@jointhetapestry.com from your account's address and we'll delete the account for you.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Edit your profile in Settings at any time.
  • Block anyone; report anything.
  • Turn notifications off, and take back camera, microphone, photo or contacts access, in iOS Settings.
  • Delete your account in Settings.

Wherever you live, you can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal information we hold about you and give you a copy;
  • correct it;
  • delete it.

Email privacy@jointhetapestry.com from your account's address. We'll confirm it's you, and answer within 45 days. If we say no and you disagree, reply and ask us to look again. We won't treat you differently for making a request. Someone you authorise may ask for you; we'll check with you first.

We don't sell personal information and we don't share it for cross-context or targeted advertising, so there is nothing to opt out of. Because we don't track you across other websites or apps, our website does the same thing whether or not your browser sends a "Do Not Track" or Global Privacy Control signal.

8. Age

Tapestry is for people 18 and older. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18, including children under 13. If we learn we have, we'll delete the account. If you believe someone under 18 is using Tapestry, tell us at safety@jointhetapestry.com.

9. Security

We protect your data with access controls in our database, private storage that serves files through short-lived signed links, and encryption in transit. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your information, we'll tell you as the law requires.

10. Where we operate

Tapestry is run from, and offered in, the United States, and your information is processed there. If you use Tapestry from another country, your information is transferred to and handled in the United States, whose privacy laws may differ from yours.

11. Changes

If we make a material change, we'll tell you in the app before it takes effect. The date at the top shows when this policy last changed.
