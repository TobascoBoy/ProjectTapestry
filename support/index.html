Report or block someone

  • A message in a chat: hold a message from someone else and choose Report or Block.
  • A scrap in a chat: tap it to open it full screen and choose Report. In a chat of three or more, hold the person's name above what they sent for Report and Block.
  • The person you are chatting with: tap the three dots at the top of the chat.
  • On a tile: open the tile, tap the three dots beside the back arrow, and choose Report or Block people.
  • In Add friends: hold the person's row.

Blocking someone hides what they send from you and stops them from sending to you. We review reports and remove content or accounts that break the Community Guidelines.

Delete your account

Open your profile, tap Edit Profile, scroll to the bottom and choose Delete Account. This deletes your account, your scraps, the scraps you placed on tiles and your chats with individual friends. It does not remove tiles that other members already saved to their own phones. It cannot be undone. If you can't get into the app, write to privacy@jointhetapestry.com from the e-mail address on the account and we'll delete it for you.

Common questions

  • Scraps and messages disappeared from a chat. Anything that nobody collected is deleted 24 hours after everyone in the chat has opened it. Double-tap a scrap or a message to collect it into the chat's stack, where it stays.
  • Notifications aren't arriving. Check Settings → Notifications → Tapestry on your phone, then Settings inside the app.
  • The widget. Widgets need iOS 26. Add one from your home screen: hold an empty spot, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and choose Tapestry.
  • The camera is dark. Restart your phone. If the screen is being shared or mirrored, the camera is switched off by iOS until that stops.
