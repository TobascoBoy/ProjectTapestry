Tapestry Community Guidelines

Tapestry is for collecting moments with people you know, and it is for adults: you must be 18 or older. These rules keep it that way. They apply to everything on Tapestry, including what you send privately in a chat. Breaking them can get content removed and accounts banned. Serious breaches are banned on the first offence.

Allowed

  • Photos of your life: friends, trips, food, pets, parties, the beach, the gym. Swimwear is fine.
  • Jokes, swearing, and the usual chaos of a group chat, as long as nobody's being targeted.

Not allowed

Sexual content and nudity. No nudity or sexual content, including drawings and "jokes".

Intimate images without consent. Never share intimate or sexual images of anyone who hasn't agreed. We remove these within 48 hours of a valid request and ban the account.

Anything involving minors. No sexual or exploitative content involving anyone under 18, ever. We report it to the authorities.

Harassment and bullying. Don't target people with insults, threats, humiliation or unwanted contact. Blocking exists, and trying to get around a block is itself a violation.

Hate. No slurs, symbols or attacks on people for their race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, caste, sexual orientation, sex, gender identity, disability or serious illness.

Violence and self-harm. No threats, glorified violence, or content encouraging self-harm or suicide. If you or someone you know is struggling, call or text 988 (U.S.).

Other people's photos and work. Only post what you have the right to post. Don't use someone's photo to embarrass them. Don't upload copyrighted work you don't have permission to use.

Private information. Don't share someone's home address, phone number, documents or other private details without their permission.

Illegal things. Don't use Tapestry to buy, sell or arrange anything illegal, or to help anyone break the law.

Impersonation, spam and scams. Be yourself. No fake accounts, mass unsolicited sends, or schemes.

Getting around the rules. Don't make a new account after a ban, and don't try to get around a block, a limit or our safety checks.

How we enforce

  • Report anything in the app: the "…" on a profile, chat or scrap, or by holding a message, or hold a scrap on a tile. Reports are confidential.
  • Block anyone. They aren't told.
  • Before a photo or video is uploaded, the app checks it on your phone for nudity. Some words are refused in names and messages. Neither check is perfect, so reports matter.
  • We review reports and aim to act within 24 hours.
  • Depending on severity, we remove content, warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently ban.
  • Removing a scrap removes it everywhere it was copied or edited in Tapestry, and stops the same image being uploaded again.
  • If you think we got a decision wrong, write to safety@jointhetapestry.com within 30 days and we'll take a second look.

If someone is in immediate danger, call 911 or your local emergency number. We can't respond to emergencies.
