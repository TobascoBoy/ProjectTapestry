What is Tapestry

We don't have time to pretend who we are with the people we love. Some moments don't fit in a photo album. They live in the way the light hits the trees just right, the joke nobody else would get, the feeling in your chest when you were all together. Tapestry is a living digital scrapbook built for that.

Point your camera at anything — a face, your dog, your lunch — and tap to cut it out. We call them scraps. Outline them, draw on them, fuse a few together, and keep them in your collection.

Send scraps to your friends in a chat or a circle. Double-tap the ones worth keeping and they join a stack you build together. What nobody collects disappears 24 hours after it's opened.

Arrange your scraps into a Tapestry you share, and put it on each other's home screens. It's nostalgia you can touch. A feeling made visible.

Features

01  —  Scraps

Cut out anything.
Keep the moment.

Point your camera at anything — your dog, your lunch, your roommate — and tap to cut it out. It works on photos and videos.

Add an outline, draw on it, or fuse a few together. We call them scraps, and the ones you save go into your collection.

02  —  Chats

Trade scraps
with your friends.

Send scraps and messages to one friend or a whole circle of them. Double-tap the ones worth keeping to collect them into a stack you build together.

Give a gold star to the best ones — you only get a few each week, so they mean something. What nobody collects disappears 24 hours after it's opened.

sent back and forth, gathered into a stack, leafed through

03  —  Circles

Your people,
in one circle.

A circle is a group chat for the friends you choose. Pick them, name it if you like, and everyone is added to one chat the moment you start it.

Everyone sees what's sent from then on and collects into the same stack. Anyone can leave.

04  —  Tapestries

A scrapbook
you grow together.

Start a Tapestry from any chat. Arrange your scraps into collages, and each one becomes a tile in a Tapestry you share with the people in that chat.

Come back to it, add to it, and watch it fill up with the little things from your days. Only the people in it can see it.

05  —  Widgets

On each other's
home screens.

Add the Tapestry widget and your shared collages live on your home screen. When a friend adds to one, it shows up on everyone's.

The widget requires iOS 26.

Nostalgia

noun  ·  coined 1688
from Greek nostos + algos

1. Originally, a literal and often physical pain caused by an intense desire to return home; homesickness.

2. A sentimental longing or wistful affection for the past, typically for a period or place with happy personal associations.

Root word breakdown

νόστος
nostos Return home, homecoming, or native land.
ἄλγος
algos Pain, suffering, ache, or distress.

Scrapbooking is about taking nostalgia, the emotions you feel, and the memories you have, and processing them artistically. It’s important to process your past; in a way, it can help ground you in the present.

Our Mission

Restoring the art
of the scrapbook.

Tapestry is restoring and refreshing the tradition of scrapbooking in the digital age with a collaborative touch. Scrapbooking has been a part of American history since its inception. People like Abraham Lincoln and Mark Twain were passionate scrapbookers. It has persisted as a hallmark of creative minds and individual thinkers for hundreds of years.

A nearly forgotten art: the dawn of the iPhone and the full digitalization of photos has left the art of stitching your photos into breathing memories by the wayside. While many still scrapbook, the number of photos at your fingertips to weave together has been significantly diminished. Tapestry seeks to help us tap into our massive libraries of digital photos and unpack their true value.

A note from Luke

I am a very nostalgic person, and I often get trapped in my dreams of the past: a song that I used to play all the time or an unforgettable camping trip. I loop myself back over and over again in my head. Tapestry is a project near and dear to my heart because I see it as a way to help process my nostalgia and ground myself in the present.

I think that a lot of people in my generation feel the same way. I see my generation as being in a crisis of identity. We are trapped in a rapidly changing world, stuck dreaming about our past, while looking towards the future with uncertainty. Tapestry is intended to be a therapeutic way to take nostalgia from our past and weave it into a memory in our present. It allows us to come together and see the way our friends reflect on the same world.

I hope that my passion project can bring as much joy to you as it has brought to me!

Luke Hattrup
