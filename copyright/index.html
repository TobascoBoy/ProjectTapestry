Copyright, Intimate-Image Removal & Legal Requests

1. Remove an intimate image of you (TAKE IT DOWN Act)

If an intimate image or video of you is on Tapestry and you didn't consent to it being shared, we'll remove it, and identical copies we know of, within 48 hours of a valid request. This includes images that were digitally created or altered to look like you. You don't need an account, and it costs nothing.

Email safety@jointhetapestry.com with:

  1. Your name, and your physical or electronic signature. Typing your full name counts.
  2. A description of the image, so we can identify it.
  3. Where it is: the Tapestry username of whoever posted or sent it, and anything else that helps us find it.
  4. A brief statement that you believe in good faith the image was shared without your consent.
  5. How to contact you.

If someone is acting for you, they should say so. We may contact you to confirm. We don't tell the person who posted the image who made the request.

If you are in danger, contact local emergency services. If the person shown is under 18, also report to NCMEC at report.cybertip.org.

2. Copyright (DMCA)

We respond to notices of claimed copyright infringement under 17 U.S.C. § 512.

Designated agent: Luke Richard Hattrup 1292 High St, Unit # A3258, Eugene, OR 97401 (541) 632-3765 copyright@jointhetapestry.com

A notice must include:

  1. Your physical or electronic signature.
  2. Identification of the copyrighted work you say is infringed.
  3. Identification of the material on Tapestry, and enough information for us to find it (for example the username and a description).
  4. Your contact information.
  5. A statement that you have a good-faith belief the use is not authorised by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
  6. A statement that the notice is accurate and, under penalty of perjury, that you are the owner or authorised to act for them.

What we do with a notice. If it is complete, we remove or disable the material promptly and tell the person who posted it, including a copy of the notice.

Counter-notice. If your content was removed and you believe that was a mistake or that you have the right to use it, send a counter-notice to the same address. It must include:

  • your signature;
  • identification of the removed material and where it was;
  • a statement under penalty of perjury that you believe in good faith it was removed by mistake or misidentification;
  • your name, address and phone number;
  • consent to the jurisdiction of the federal district court for your address (or the District of Oregon if you're outside the U.S.), and that you'll accept service from the person who sent the notice.

We forward a valid counter-notice to the claimant. We restore the material in 10–14 business days unless they tell us they've filed a court action to stop the use.

Repeat infringers. Accounts with three upheld copyright notices are permanently closed. See the Terms, section 7.

Misrepresentation. Knowingly false notices or counter-notices can make you liable for damages (17 U.S.C. § 512(f)).

3. Other safety concerns

Report inside the app (the "…" menus, or hold a scrap on a tile), or email safety@jointhetapestry.com. We aim to act within 24 hours. This includes a photo of you that was posted without your permission, or someone pretending to be you. You don't need an account to write to us.

4. Law enforcement

Requests should name the account (username or email) and the legal basis. Preservation requests under 18 U.S.C. § 2703(f) are honoured.
